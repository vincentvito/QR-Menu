## Prioritization

### Scoring rubric

Each category is scored from 0 to its maximum weight; total is out of 100.

- Product fit (30): current product can satisfy the intent truthfully and completely.
- Conversion proximity (25): distance from query to creating/importing a menu and starting a trial.
- Demonstrated demand (20): Search Console demand when available; for this baseline only, breadth and consistency of competing live results is a clearly labeled proxy.
- Original proof (15): Qtable can provide its own screenshots, workflow, samples, or measurements.
- Effort/competition (10): higher means a narrower, more feasible result set and lower production effort.
