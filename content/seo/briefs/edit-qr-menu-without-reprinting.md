## SERP observations and angle

Competitor pages repeatedly promise live updates and no reprints. Make this concrete with a truthful edit-preview-publish-scan workflow, not generic dynamic-QR jargon. Distinguish updating the linked menu from changing the QR design or destination.

## Proof and flagged claims

- Original sequence showing a price edit and the same QR resolving to the update.
- Verified publication/subscription behavior and QR customization behavior.
- **FLAG:** Verify cache/update timing before saying “instantly”; do not promise indefinite QR operation outside documented account/subscription behavior.
