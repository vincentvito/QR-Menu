# Brief: QR menu launch checklist

- Status: approved supporting page
- Canonical: `/blog/qr-menu-launch-checklist`
- Target user/job: a restaurant owner or manager preparing to print and place QR cards.
- Primary intent/query: informational, bottom funnel: “QR menu launch checklist.”
- Funnel/CTA: bottom funnel; CTA “Build and test your QR menu.”
- Author/reviewer: hospitality content writer / product and accessibility reviewer.

## Questions to answer

- What must be checked before printing many table cards?
- Which phones, cameras, lighting, distances, and network conditions should be tested?
- How should menu content, prices, dietary information, and languages be reviewed?
- What fallback should guests have?
- How are post-launch scans and menu changes monitored?

## SERP observations and angle

How-to results emphasize generation and placement, often lightly covering the actual diner experience. Build an operational checklist from Qtable’s own launch requirements: production URL, real-device scan, template review, content QA, scannability, and a fallback. Avoid unsupported claims about scan uplift.

## Proof and flagged claims

- Original printable checklist and test table (device, distance, lighting, network, result).
- Original examples of readable CTA/placement and an intentionally poor example.
- Verified QR download formats and current analytics definitions.
- **FLAG:** Any minimum size, distance, contrast ratio, or “best placement” number needs a cited standard or controlled test.
