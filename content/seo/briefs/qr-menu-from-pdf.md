# Brief: QR menu from PDF

- Status: approved first-cluster hub
- Canonical: `/qr-menu-from-pdf`
- Target user/job: a restaurant owner with an existing PDF who wants a table-ready QR menu without manually retyping every dish.
- Primary intent/query: transactional — “convert PDF menu to QR code.”
- Secondary queries: “upload restaurant menu PDF create QR code,” “convert restaurant menu to digital menu.”
- Funnel/CTA: bottom funnel; primary CTA “Upload your menu,” secondary CTA “View a sample mobile menu.”
- Author/reviewer: product marketing / product owner and SEO reviewer.

## Questions to answer

- Does Qtable host the PDF or turn its contents into editable dishes?
- Which file types and limits apply?
- Can the owner correct extraction errors before publishing?
- Will future edits require a new QR code?
- What does a diner see on a phone?

## SERP observations and angle

Current results are mostly PDF-hosting generators, flipbooks, and upload tools promising a QR in seconds. Qtable’s useful distinction is converting existing content into editable categories and dishes displayed as a branded mobile menu. Explain the review/publish steps honestly; do not promise perfect extraction or an unsupported completion time.

## Proof required before publication

- Original source-PDF and resulting editor/public-menu screenshots.
- Short original recording showing one corrected price behind the same QR.
- Verified supported formats, maximum file count/size, and publish/trial requirements.
- **FLAG:** Validate any speed, accuracy, “instant,” accessibility, or performance claim with a reproducible test.
