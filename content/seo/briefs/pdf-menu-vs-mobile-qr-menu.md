## SERP observations and angle

Search results mix PDF upload hosts with mobile-first menu builders. Diner feedback specifically criticizes desktop-sized PDFs on phones. Use a fair decision table, acknowledge cases where a simple PDF is adequate, and show how Qtable bridges the migration by importing the PDF.

- **FLAG:** Accessibility, load-time, SEO, and conversion claims require measured evidence and qualified wording; one anecdotal article is not universal user research.
